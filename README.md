## About The Game

**Before The Colours Fade** is a narrative turn-based deckbuilder infused with psychological 2D exploration.

Grounded in the theme of **"Inner Childhood Dream"**, the game chronicles the emotional journey of a young man confronting the weight of adulthood, grief, and isolation. When the reality of everyday life becomes unbearable, he shifts into a world shaped by his childhood imagination. In this realm, real-life anxieties and burdens manifest as fantasy beasts that must be overcome through strategic, card-based combat.


---


## Synopsis and Lore

Hilbert Hickman (20 years old) was once an eccentric young man brimming with wild imagination. Alongside his closest friend, **L.G.**, the two were inseparable—like two sides of the same coin. Hilbert provided the analytical mind, drafting intricate schematics and mechanical inventions, while his friend brought infectious optimism and creative heart to every dream they pursued.

Everything fractured a year ago when a tragic accident claimed his friend's life.

Now, Hilbert is a shadow of his former self. Confined to a cramped 12-square-meter apartment, he stares at peeling walls and a leaking ceiling, avoids rent demands from his landlady, and dreads an upcoming university examination worth half his academic grade. To him, the world has bled of color—bleak, quiet, and cold.

> As panic paralyzes him in the faculty hallway, words from the past echo in his memory:
> _"When it's too loud, just treat it like a game."_

Closing his eyes, Hilbert shifts beyond the veil of reality into the **Child World**—a nostalgic, hand-drawn fantasy landscape constructed from his childhood notebooks. Reunited with **n.n.**, a small propeller-driven automaton from his early schematics, Hilbert discovers that overcoming his struggles in reality begins with confronting them in the realm of dreams.

## Two Interconnected Worlds

_Before The Colours Fade_ bridges two contrasting audiovisual and psychological dimensions:


### 1. The Reality World

- **Atmosphere:** Grayscale visual palette, heavy graphite textures, oppressive shadows, and slow movement.
- **Audio:** Low-pass filtered room hums, heavy footfalls, and the steady, ticking cadence of an apartment clock resembling a nervous heartbeat.
- **Function:** Serves as the narrative grounding ground where Hilbert navigates daily routines, discovers memory triggers, and confronts tangible sources of stress.


### 2. The Child World

- **Atmosphere:** Warm sienna, gold parchment tones, dynamic hand-drawn linework, and open skies.
- **Audio:** Sharp, melodic acoustic guitar phrases, tactile paper-tear sound effects, and rhythmic clockwork clicks.
- **Function:** The primary combat arena where psychological conflicts materialize into encounters.

## Gameplay Mechanics

```
+--------------------+         Dimension Shift        +--------------------+
|   REALITY WORLD    |  ===========================>  |    CHILD WORLD     |
| - 2D Exploration   |                                | - Deckbuilder      |
| - Uncover Stressors|  <===========================  | - Gather Energy    |
| - Grounding Buffs  |       Use Dream Fragments      | - Slay Monsters    |
+--------------------+                                +--------------------+
```


### 1. Interactive 2D Narrative Exploration

Players navigate Hilbert's apartment, desolate university corridors, and tense lecture rooms. Environmental interactions provide more than narrative context:

- Engaging with empathetic peers or inspecting past blueprints grants mental clarity, translating into combat advantages such as additional initial Block or bonus card draws.

### 3. Tactical Turn-Based Deckbuilding

Combat operates on a turn-based system fueled by **Clockwork Energy Gears**:

- **Attack Cards:** Strike, Heavy Slash, and Double Strike to chip away at enemy health.
- **Defense Cards:** Defend, Fortress, and Counter Stance to deflect incoming harm.
- **Special Cards:** Unleash childhood power fantasies like Fireball and Thunder.

<img width="1322" height="750" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-04 151132" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dfc42d4e-03b2-4bc4-8d1f-3d1c470e0bf6" />

#### Hand Control and Anti-RNG Features:

To prevent dead hands and reward foresight, players possess dedicated tactical commands each round:

- **Reroll:** Cycle an unneeded card back into the deck for an immediate replacement (Usable once per round).
- **Retain:** Lock an essential card in hand, preserving it for the following turn instead of sending it to the discard pile (Usable once per round).
